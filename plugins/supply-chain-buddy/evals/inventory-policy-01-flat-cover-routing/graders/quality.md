PASS only if ALL of the following hold:
1. The response explains that a flat weeks-of-cover rule ignores demand variability and/or lead time (and therefore over-protects stable or slow items and under-protects volatile ones).
2. It recommends segmenting the assortment (e.g. ABC by value and XYZ by variability, or a comparable multi-criteria segmentation) with differentiated service targets.
3. It recommends sizing safety stock statistically from demand (or forecast error) variability and lead time (and lead time variability), not as a fixed number of weeks.
4. It names concrete data it needs from the user (at least demand history per SKU and lead times; unit cost or value is a plus).

FAIL if the main recommendation is to raise safety stock (or weeks of cover) uniformly for all items.
FAIL if it invents company-specific figures or presents industry benchmark statistics as facts without labelling them as assumptions or rules of thumb.
