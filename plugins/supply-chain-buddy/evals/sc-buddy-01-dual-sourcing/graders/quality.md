PASS only if ALL of the following hold:
1. Frames the decision explicitly (e.g. what problem dual sourcing should solve, criteria, who decides) rather than jumping to yes/no.
2. Weighs supply risk/resilience against cost effects such as total cost of ownership (e.g. qualification/tooling cost, volume leverage, price), and mentions at least one alternative to full dual sourcing (e.g. safety stock/buffer, supplier development, qualified backup supplier, split volumes).
3. Asks for or lists concrete data needed (e.g. delivery performance history, lead times, cost/price data, switching/tooling cost, demand volume, impact of late deliveries).
4. Does not invent specific cost, risk or savings figures as facts.

FAIL if it gives a definitive recommendation without stating the trade-off or the information it depends on.
